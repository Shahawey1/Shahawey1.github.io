04 / SELECTED WORKPEOPLE STRATEGY IN PRACTICE

From HR priorities
to business practice.

Selected professional case studies showing how I structure people challenges into practical frameworks, governance, and measurable review processes. Examples are presented without confidential employer information.

01 / PERFORMANCEPEOPLE · PERFORMANCE · EVIDENCE

Building a KPI-Based Performance Management Framework

Moving performance reviews beyond subjective ratings toward clear expectations, evidence-based measurement, and consistent review practices.

THE APPROACH

Structure role-relevant KPIs and behavioral competencies, clarify measurement methods and data sources, define review responsibilities, and introduce validation checks for appraisal results.

KEY DELIVERABLES

KPI libraries and forms · rating guidance · evidence requirements · review-cycle governance · result assurance checks · manager awareness materials.

BUSINESS VALUE

A more transparent and consistent basis for performance conversations, development decisions, and management reporting.

02 / TALENTACQUISITION · DEVELOPMENT · SUCCESSION

Connecting Talent Acquisition with Employee Development

Bringing recruitment, onboarding, learning, performance feedback, and succession support into a connected talent-management approach.

THE APPROACH

Align manpower needs and job requirements with structured candidate assessment; support onboarding follow-up; identify learning needs; and use performance and competency insights to inform development priorities.

KEY DELIVERABLES

Recruitment workflow and interview assessment tools · onboarding checkpoints · training-needs inputs · development plans · competency and succession support.

BUSINESS VALUE

Clearer talent decisions and a more deliberate connection between hiring, capability building, and internal talent readiness.

03 / ORGANIZATIONSTRUCTURE · ROLE CLARITY · GOVERNANCE

Strengthening Organizational Design and HR Governance

Supporting organizational effectiveness by clarifying responsibilities, aligning roles, and making HR processes easier to execute and govern.

THE APPROACH

Review organizational requirements, support structure and job-description alignment, define process ownership, and distinguish local execution from group-level standards, validation, and approval.

KEY DELIVERABLES

Organization and role reviews · job-description alignment · process maps and SOP responsibilities · HR governance and approval frameworks.

BUSINESS VALUE

Stronger role clarity, more consistent process execution, and clearer accountability across teams and organizational levels.

These case studies describe the nature of my professional work. Quantified outcomes can be added when validated figures are available and approved for public disclosure.
